The Participant Journey

From participant to owner.

TISIKALECH is designed as a progression, not a one-time training programme. Each stage builds the capability, experience and economic position needed for the next.

01

Select

Identify women with the commitment and potential to operate an enterprise.

02

Train

Build practical skills in production, hygiene, costing, sales, finance and digital tools.

03

Operate

Run the venture with structured support, standards and measurable targets.

04

Earn

Receive a defined share of business profit as performance and enterprise value grow.

05

Own

Progress toward ownership through demonstrated performance and agreed transition terms.

06

Franchise

Proven operators can grow into owners of additional locations.

06

The end goal is
not dependency.
